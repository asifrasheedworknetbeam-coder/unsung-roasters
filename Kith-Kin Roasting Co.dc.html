Our Story

Kith+Kin Roasting Co. has been a part of the coffee industry since 2005. We’ve had our share of great years and challenging ones, weathered the storm of COVID shutdowns, and overcome countless obstacles along the way. Through it all, our unwavering commitment to quality and our passion for roasting have kept us going—earning us a loyal following that extends well beyond our small hometown of Artesia, NM.

We carefully monitor every roast, taking precise readings to ensure consistency and uphold our strict quality standards. From California to New York, our coffees reach customers who truly appreciate the care and craftsmanship that goes into every batch. Even with local roasters in their own cities, they continue to choose us—something that speaks volumes about the quality of our beans.

Try Kith+Kin Roasting Co. and experience for yourself why our customers keep coming back for more.

From Jahva House to Kith+Kin: A Legacy of Friends, Family, and Fresh Beginnings

At 7am on Monday, November 21, 2005, The Jahva House turned on its open sign for the first time. Despite it being a soft opening, we had people waiting at the door with their faces pressed on the window…and they still are. The Jahva House has been a much-needed social center for Artesia since day one.

A lot has happened since then, good and bad. We’ve seen some things. And then, 13 years later, we saw a big change: the addition of an in-house coffee roastery, bakery and meeting area. With that came our new identity, Kith+Kin Roasting Co.

‘Kith + Kin’ is an Old English phrase meaning acquaintances and relatives. Our friends and family. This is the foundation of our business. We wouldn’t exist without it.

From my family who put up the building, my friends who volunteered during construction, relatives who helped us through some tough times, and now the long-time friends of The Jahva House who have assisted us in making this fresh start, we wouldn’t still be here without any of them. So, this new beginning and fresh start is dedicated to our friends and family, our Kith and Kin.
