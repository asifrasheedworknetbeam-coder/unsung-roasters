Our Story

“I was previously incarcerated.”

That’s not an easy statement to proclaim, but one that our Founder, Rhonda Bear, is more than happy to share. From an early age, Rhonda struggled with drug addiction, resulting in numerous arrests, losing custody of her children, and serving prison time. After regaining custody of her kids, Rhonda began working with other organizations in the area to uplift and encourage women like herself through the transition from incarceration back into society.

She later founded She Brews as a way to provide stable employment and housing, life skills, and mentors to walk alongside women. Her perseverance and dedication has made She Brews what it is today– a growing coffee house, roastery, and transition program dedicated to disrupting cycles of incarceration and restoring families.

We use quality coffee to open doors and create second chances for women transitioning out of incarceration. Our programs and employment opportunities give women the skills, community support, and inspiration to break the cycle of incarceration and reunite with their children. At She Brews, we’re serving truly transformative coffee.

Transition Program

With no money, no job, and not to mention a felony record, most landlords don’t want to take on this population. Helping a woman find a safe and stable place to sleep is the first step to getting her life back on track.

At She Brews, we provide safe and affordable residential housing and program support for women transitioning out of incarceration across Oklahoma. Our housing program includes group homes, family homes for women reuniting with their children, and graduate homes for women who need extended support.

Incarceration

Women discover She Brews while incarcerated and are met “at the gate” when accepted into the program.

Housing

She Brews provides safe, affordable residential housing combined with structured program support for the women we serve.

Employment

Our nonprofit coffee shops serve as social enterprises, offering employment opportunities and fostering essential job skills and leadership development.

Mentors

Dedicated volunteers organize into small groups to guide women through their transition to a healthier life, offering relational, community, and spiritual support.

Reunification

She Brews equips and assists the women we serve in reuniting with their children, including support for court appearances and family housing options.

Graduation

Upon completing our program, each client is ready to lead a successful and independent life within the community.

We’re Pouring Out Hope

She Brews began with the mission to lift up women coming out of incarceration, providing them with a safe, supportive environment to live in recovery and develop new skills to craft a new life. We live that mission every day, and we embrace the stories of the women in our shops as sources of hope. Change is always possible when you wake up and do good.
