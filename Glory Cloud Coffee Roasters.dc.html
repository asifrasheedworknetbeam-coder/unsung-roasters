From Roaster to Cup

What We Do

Glory Cloud serves individuals, cafés and wholesale partners in five ways, from roasting the beans to helping you brew them well.

01

Craft-Roasted Coffee

We roast coffee for individuals and wholesale clients, pairing science with artisan skill and the best available beans.

02

Cold Brew on Tap

Cold brew on tap, with or without nitrogen, for restaurants and cafés, plus a bottled ready-to-drink line in several flavors.

03

Loose-Leaf Tea

Loose-leaf tea packaged for wholesale, cafés, in-store retail and online orders.

04

Coffee & Tea Training

Hands-on lessons at our roastery, often on the spot for anyone who walks through the door.

05

Quality Consulting

We test your water, grind and extraction, then help you tune your equipment for the best flavor in every cup.
