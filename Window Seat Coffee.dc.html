Our Story

Most people experience moments each day when they would just like to get away for a little while. Sound familiar? Window Seat Coffee aims to provide the inspiration (and caffeine) needed to do just that.

It may come as a surprise, but the idea for Window Seat Coffee was born in the cubicles of public accounting. It was during this time in our life that we dreamed of a fresh start and a new adventure. One day, while out on our daily coffee run, it hit us: getting out of the office and taking even a small break was the positive moment we needed to feel refreshed and ready for the rest of the day.

We wanted to make that simple, yet relatable, feeling the spirit of our new business. Whether on a quick coffee run, away with the family at the lake, or on an epic trip around the world, we have all experienced the energizing feeling of getting away from it all.

— Tom & Kristen
